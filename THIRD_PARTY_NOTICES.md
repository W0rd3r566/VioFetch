## Fastfetch ASCII logo artwork

The ASCII logos for Android, Arch Linux, CachyOS, Debian, Fedora, Manjaro, Ubuntu, Windows, macOS, and generic Linux bundled in viofetch.py are adapted from the Fastfetch project:

- Project: https://github.com/fastfetch-cli/fastfetch
- Logo source directory: https://github.com/fastfetch-cli/fastfetch/tree/dev/src/logo/ascii
- Individual source files: a/android.txt, a/arch.txt, c/cachyos.txt, d/debian.txt, f/fedora.txt, m/manjaro.txt, u/ubuntu.txt, w/windows.txt, m/macos.txt, and l/linux.txt.
